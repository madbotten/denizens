================================================================================
                                    DENIZENS
                        Game Overview & Core Mechanics
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW & STRATEGIC TENSION
-------------------------------
Denizens is a cyclical, turn-based tactical battle game played on a 61-hex
flat-top hexagonal arena (radius 4). The player commands a persistent army of
reptilian creatures (up to MAX_ARMY_SIZE = 16, receiving REINFORCEMENTS_PER_ROUND
new Stage 1 creatures after each round) across a series of battles against
mammalian enemies.

Surviving battles allows player creatures to evolve through 8 increasingly
powerful and specialized stages. However, if a creature is slain in battle, its
evolution is cut short permanently—creating a core strategic tension between
risking your strongest evolved veterans to win tough battles and protecting them
while training newer recruits.


2. HEX FACINGS & DIRECTIONAL COMBAT
-----------------------------------
Every creature on the flat-top hex grid has an explicit orientation ("facing")
aligned with one of the 6 sides of its hex:
  - 0: North      (0°   — top horizontal hex edge)
  - 1: North-East (60°  — top-right edge)
  - 2: South-East (120° — bottom-right edge)
  - 3: South      (180° — bottom horizontal hex edge)
  - 4: South-West (240° — bottom-left edge)
  - 5: North-West (300° — top-left edge)

Relative to a creature's current orientation, the 6 surrounding hex directions
are:
  - Forward (Front)
  - Forward-Right (Front-Right)
  - Flank-Right
  - Rear
  - Flank-Left
  - Forward-Left (Front-Left)

Basic Combat Rules & Exceptions:
  - Basic Forms (Stage 1): Can only attack into their Forward hex and have
    vulnerable flanks (Flank-Left, Flank-Right) and Rear when attacked from
    those directions.
  - Evolved Forms: Higher stages modify offensive and defensive arcs (for
    example, the Stage 5 Amphisbaena attacks both Forward and Rear and has no
    Rear vulnerability; the Stage 8 Hydra, Young Dragon, Tian Long, and Dragon
    Turtle attack across Forward, Forward-Left, and Forward-Right; and high-tier
    Turtles armor their flanks and rear).


3. PLAYER CREATURE CLASSES vs. ENEMIES
--------------------------------------
Player creatures belong to three reptilian classes, each with a distinct
tactical identity:

  1. Snakes (Serpents) : High attack, fragile defense.
  2. Lizards           : High mobility, weak early attack. Capitalize on
                         mobility with extra Rotate-Move steps, wider rotation
                         angles, multi-directional movement, and flight.
  3. Turtles           : High defense, low mobility armored tanks.

Enemies (Mammals — Do Not Evolve):
  - Opposing forces consist of 13 Mammal enemy forms encountered across the
    cyclical battle progression:
      1. Rat
      2. Wolf
      3. Bear
      4. Lion
      5. Griffon
      6. Owlbear
      7. Behemoth
      8. Displacer Beast
      9. Manticore
     10. Rajang
     11. Dire Wolf
     12. Sabertooth
     13. Hell Hound


4. ATTRIBUTE-DRIVEN MOVEMENT, ROTATION, FLIGHT & ATTACK JUNCTURES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Rather than hardcoding movement rules, every creature form defines its tactical
capabilities through five core attributes:

  - num_rotate_moves      : Number of (Rotate -> Move) cycles allowed per turn.
  - max_rotate_per_move   : Maximum 60° hex faces (0..3) the creature may rotate
                            from its step-start facing during each cycle.
  - legal_move_dirs       : Set of allowed movement directions relative to the
                            creature's instantaneous facing at the moment of the
                            move (e.g. {Forward} for Basic Stage 1 creatures;
                            {Forward, Forward-Left, Forward-Right, Rear} for
                            Stage 8 Tian Long).
  - total_attacks_per_move: Total attacks allowed during the creature's turn
                            (default 1; up to 2 or 3 for apex evolutions).
  - can_fly               : Winged creatures (Jaculus, Wyrm, Wyrmling, Drake,
                            Young Dragon, Tian Long, Griffon, Manticore) can fly
                            over occupied hexes during intermediate move steps
                            as long as they can land on an empty hex before
                            their turn ends.

Optional Actions & Attack Junctures:
  - Within each Rotate-Move cycle, there are three junctures:
      1. BEFORE_ROTATE
      2. AFTER_ROTATE
      3. AFTER_MOVE
  - Rotations are optional, moves are optional, and attacks are optional.
  - A creature may take at most 1 attack per juncture, up to its
    total_attacks_per_move limit across its turn.
  - Example (Basic Stage 1 Creature):
      num_rotate_moves = 1, max_rotate_per_move = 1, legal_move_dirs = {Forward}.
      Can rotate at most 1 hex face left or right from its starting facing and
      move 1 hex Forward, once per turn.
  - Example (Stage 8 Tian Long):
      num_rotate_moves = 4, max_rotate_per_move = 3,
      legal_move_dirs = {Forward, Forward-Left, Forward-Right, Rear}, can_fly = True.
      Can rotate to any of the 6 hex facings (up to 3 faces = 180°) before each
      of its 4 moves, moving in any of its 4 allowed relative directions on each
      step and flying over occupied hexes along the way.


5. EVOLUTION SYSTEM (8 STAGES PER CLASS)
----------------------------------------
Each of the three player classes has 8 evolution stages (Stage 1 to Stage 8).
Creatures evolve by surviving battles. Advancing from Stage N to Stage N+1
requires surviving N additional battles (triangular number progression):

  - Stage 1 -> Stage 2 : Survive 1 battle       (1 total battle survived)
  - Stage 2 -> Stage 3 : Survive 2 more battles (3 total battles survived)
  - Stage 3 -> Stage 4 : Survive 3 more battles (6 total battles survived)
  - Stage 4 -> Stage 5 : Survive 4 more battles (10 total battles survived)
  - Stage 5 -> Stage 6 : Survive 5 more battles (15 total battles survived)
  - Stage 6 -> Stage 7 : Survive 6 more battles (21 total battles survived)
  - Stage 7 -> Stage 8 : Survive 7 more battles (28 total battles survived)

6. CONTROLS & INTERFACE
-----------------------
  - Left-Click Unit          : Select unit and inspect its portrait, stats, and
                               legal moves/rotations.
  - Wheel / Left-Right / Q-E : Rotate selected unit 60° counter-clockwise or
                               clockwise within max_rotate_per_move.
  - Right-Click Adjacent Hex : Rotate selected unit toward an adjacent hex.
  - Left-Click Highlight Hex : Move selected unit into a legal destination hex
                               (green = empty hex, cyan = fly-over occupied hex).
  - F Key                    : Execute/test attack at current juncture
                               (BEFORE_ROTATE / AFTER_ROTATE / AFTER_MOVE).
  - Space Key                : End current team's turn.
  - [ / ] Keys (DEBUG=True)  : Cycle selected unit's evolution stage (1..8) or
                               Mammal enemy form (1..13).
