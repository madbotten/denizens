================================================================================
                                    DENIZENS
                        Game Overview & Core Mechanics
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW
-----------
Denizens is a cyclical, turn-based tactical battle game played on a hexagonal
grid. The player commands a team of reptilian creatures across a series of
battles against various mammalian enemies. Surviving battles allows creatures
to evolve through 8 increasingly powerful and specialized stages.


2. HEX FACINGS & DIRECTIONAL COMBAT
-----------------------------------
Every creature on the hex grid has an explicit orientation ("facing") aligned
with one of the 6 sides of its hex. Relative to a creature's current orientation,
the 6 surrounding hex directions are:

  - Front        (forward facing)
  - Front-Left
  - Front-Right
  - Flank-Left
  - Flank-Right
  - Rear

Basic Combat Rules & Exceptions:
  - Basic Forms (Stage 1): Can only attack into their Front hex and have
    vulnerable flanks (Flank-Left, Flank-Right) and Rear when attacked from
    those directions.
  - Evolved Forms: Many higher stages break or modify standard facing rules
    for offense or defense (for example, the Stage 8 Hydra can attack toward
    Front, Front-Left, and Front-Right).


3. PLAYER CREATURE CLASSES vs. ENEMIES
--------------------------------------
Player creatures belong to three reptilian classes, each with a distinct
tactical identity:

  1. Snakes (Serpents) : High attack, fragile defense.
  2. Lizards           : High mobility, weak attack. Capitalize on mobility by
                         moving in directions other than their Front facing and
                         by choosing the order of Move and Attack actions.
  3. Turtles           : High defense, low mobility tanks.

Enemies (Mammals — Do Not Evolve):
  - Opposing forces consist of 13 Mammal enemy forms encountered across the
    cyclical battle progression:
      1. Rat
      2. Wolf
      3. Bear
      4. Lion
      5. Griffon
      6. Owlbear
      7. Behemoth
      8. Displacer Beast
      9. Manticore
     10. Rajang
     11. Dire Wolf
     12. Sabertooth
     13. Hell Hound


4. TURN-BASED MOVEMENT & ACTION RULES
-------------------------------------
Battles are turn-based. Creature movement is divided into two distinct parts:
  - Rotations : Changing the creature's hex facing.
  - Moves     : Entering new hexes on the board.

Movement Rules & Exceptions:
  - Basic Forms (Stage 1): Must move forward into the hex corresponding to
    their current Front facing.
  - Evolved & Class Exceptions: Specific classes and evolved forms override
    basic movement constraints. In particular, Lizards can move in directions
    other than their forward facing and can flexibly choose the order of their
    Move and Attack actions during a turn.


5. EVOLUTION SYSTEM (8 STAGES PER CLASS)
----------------------------------------
Each of the three player classes has 8 evolution stages (Stage 1 to Stage 8).
Creatures evolve by surviving battles. Advancing from Stage N to Stage N+1
requires surviving N additional battles (triangular number progression):

  - Stage 1 -> Stage 2 : Survive 1 battle       (1 total battle survived)
  - Stage 2 -> Stage 3 : Survive 2 more battles (3 total battles survived)
  - Stage 3 -> Stage 4 : Survive 3 more battles (6 total battles survived)
  - Stage 4 -> Stage 5 : Survive 4 more battles (10 total battles survived)
  - Stage 5 -> Stage 6 : Survive 5 more battles (15 total battles survived)
  - Stage 6 -> Stage 7 : Survive 6 more battles (21 total battles survived)
  - Stage 7 -> Stage 8 : Survive 7 more battles (28 total battles survived)
